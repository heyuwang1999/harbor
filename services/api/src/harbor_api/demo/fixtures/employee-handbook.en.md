# Harbor Bakery Employee Handbook

Fictional content for demonstration purposes. Not legal advice.

## Statutory Holidays

An employee who has been employed continuously for four weeks or more, working at least
eighteen hours per week, is entitled to statutory holidays. If you are rostered to work on a
statutory holiday, the branch manager arranges an alternative holiday within thirty days
before or after that day and records it on the roster.

## Annual Leave

Employees earn paid annual leave after twelve months of service: seven days per year for the
first two years, then one additional day per year up to a maximum of fourteen days. Leave must
be taken within three months after the end of the leave year and is not automatically paid out.

Requests go through the HR system at least fourteen days in advance and need branch manager
approval. Leave is generally not approved during peak periods: the two weeks before Lunar New
Year and the week before Mid-Autumn Festival.

## Sick Leave and Medical Certificates

An employee absent for four or more consecutive days who has accumulated enough paid sickness
days receives a sickness allowance of four-fifths of average daily wages.

A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for the sickness allowance, stating
the dates of absence and why the employee was unfit for work. Even a single sick day must be
reported to the branch manager at least one hour before the shift starts, with the certificate
handed in on the first day back.

## MPF Contributions

The employer and the employee each contribute five per cent of relevant income to the Mandatory
Provident Fund. Employees earning below HK$7,100 per month make no employee contribution, while
the employer still contributes. New joiners are enrolled within their first sixty days.

## Typhoon and Rainstorm Arrangements

When Typhoon Signal No. 8 or above is hoisted, or a Black Rainstorm Warning is issued, all
shops close and non-essential staff do not report to work. Once the signal is lowered, staff
return within two hours if at least two hours of trading remain that day.

Hours missed because of severe weather are not deducted from annual leave or wages.

## Probation and Notice

Probation lasts three months. During probation either party may terminate with seven days'
notice or payment in lieu; within the first month no notice is required.

After probation, either party must give one month's notice or payment in lieu.

## Overtime and Time Off in Lieu

Overtime needs advance approval from the branch manager. Approved overtime is compensated with
time off in lieu, which must be taken within three months. Standard hours for full-time staff
are forty-four per week.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave

Eligible female employees are entitled to fourteen weeks of continuous maternity leave. Eligible
male employees receive five days of paternity leave, which may be taken separately between four
weeks before the expected date of delivery and fourteen weeks after birth.

## Staff Benefits

- Full-time staff and immediate family receive 30% off shop products, capped at HK$1,000 monthly.
- Group medical insurance covers outpatient, hospital and basic dental check-ups for full-time staff.
- Up to HK$8,000 per year of training sponsorship is available; see the expense policy.

## Uniform and Grooming

Front-of-house staff wear the company uniform and non-slip shoes. Kitchen staff must wear hair
nets and masks. Two uniform sets are issued each year; additional replacements require branch
manager approval.
