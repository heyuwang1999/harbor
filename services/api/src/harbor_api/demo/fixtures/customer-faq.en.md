# Harbor Bakery Customer FAQ

Fictional content for demonstration purposes.

## Opening Hours

Shops open Monday to Saturday from 7:00 to 20:00, and Sunday and public holidays from 8:00 to
18:00. All shops are closed on the first three days of Lunar New Year.

## Shop Locations

- Tseung Kwan O: Shop G, 1 Wan Hang Road
- Kwun Tong: Shop 3, G/F, 88 Hoi Yuen Road
- Central: 1/F, 120 Des Voeux Road Central

## Custom Cake Orders

Custom cakes require at least three working days' notice and a 50% deposit. Photo cakes and
3D-shaped cakes need five working days. Design changes must be requested at least two working
days before collection.

## Delivery

Delivery is available in selected areas of Hong Kong Island, Kowloon and the New Territories.
Orders of HK$500 or more ship free; below that a HK$60 delivery fee applies. Deliveries run
between 10:00 and 18:00 and cannot be booked for a specific time. Outlying islands are not served.

## Refunds and Exchanges

For quality problems, bring the item and receipt back to the original shop within 24 hours of
collection for a refund or exchange. Custom cakes are made to order and cannot be refunded
except for quality issues.

## Allergen Information

All breads and cakes are made in a shared kitchen and may contain or come into contact with
wheat, egg, milk, soy and nuts. Customers with food allergies should ask staff before buying.
We cannot guarantee any product is free from a given allergen.

## Vegan and Gluten-Free Options

Three vegan breads are available (wholemeal walnut, black sesame, pumpkin seed) after 14:00 daily
at the Tseung Kwan O and Kwun Tong shops. No gluten-free products are available at this time.

## Membership Points

Members earn one point per HK$1 spent, and 100 points can be redeemed for a HK$10 cash voucher.
Points expire after twelve months. Membership and points are not transferable.

## Group and Corporate Orders

Group orders of 50 items or more receive a 10% discount. Place them in shop or by email at least
five working days before collection, with company invoicing details.

## Payment Methods

Shops accept cash, Octopus, credit cards, Alipay and WeChat Pay. Delivery orders must be paid
electronically in advance.
